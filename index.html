ℹ️ About Traffic Buddy

Traffic Buddy helps you understand Indian traffic rules and look up which challan (fine) may apply to a violation, based on your selected state.

This app provides general informational guidance, not legal advice. Central statutory penalties are shown only where a relevant provision can be identified. State compounding amounts, local traffic rules and enforcement schedules can differ and change. This build does not yet contain a fully verified, exhaustive penalty schedule for every state/UT; use the official links on each challan page before relying on an amount.

Rules and challans are organised as State × Violation records, each with a source link and last-verified date, so they can be corrected or updated without changing the app itself.

⚠️ Traffic fines and rules may change. Please verify the latest information with the official state traffic police or Government e‑Challan portal (echallan.parivahan.gov.in).
